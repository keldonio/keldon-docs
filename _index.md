Welcome to the Keldon documentation. This guide will walk you through installing the operator, deploying your first cluster, and running PostgreSQL on Kubernetes with confidence.

Keldon is a Kubernetes operator that manages the full lifecycle of PostgreSQL clusters — from provisioning and configuration to backups, failover, and observability. It is designed to be production-ready out of the box.

## What you'll find here

- **Getting Started** — Deploy your first cluster in minutes.
- **Installation** — Set up the operator on your Kubernetes cluster.
- **Configuration** — Tune PostgreSQL, storage, networking, and more.
- **High Availability** — Configure replication and automatic failover.
- **Backups** — Continuous WAL archiving and point-in-time recovery.
